# Schrödinger's Page

Until this very moment, the page you requested existed in a state of quantum superposition.

Regrettably, the act of observation (your HTTP request) has collapsed the wave function into a single, definite eigenstate: **Not Found (404)**.

Please take comfort in the knowledge that, in at least one parallel universe, you are reading this page successfully.
